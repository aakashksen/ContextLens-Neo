One meeting, seven steps

An invented planning sync, followed from speech to card.

The meeting invented
  1. t1MayaLet’s ship the beta on Friday.
  2. t2JordanDesign still owes the onboarding screens.
  3. t3JordanThen QA needs two days, minimum.
  4. t4MayaOkay, make that Monday.
  5. t5JordanI’ll send the QA plan tomorrow.
  6. t6MayaAnyone want coffee?
  1. 1

    Speech becomes turns

    Each turn arrives with the speaker unknown. A turn update names the speaker.

    • audioa chunk of speech
    • transcriptt1, speaker unknown
    • turn_updatet1, speaker Maya
  2. 2

    Memory notes what matters

    A correction links to what it replaces. “Make that Monday” is not a conflict.

    • decision
      Ship the beta on Friday.
      t1
    • correction
      Ship the beta on Monday.
      t4replaces t1
    • commitment
      Jordan sends the QA plan.
      t5
  3. 3

    The press freezes the moment

    “When does it ship?” is pressed after t5. Later turns cannot be cited.

    1. t5JordanI’ll send the QA plan tomorrow.
    2. t6MayaAnyone want coffee?

    Pressed after t5. t6 is too late to cite.

  4. 4

    Retrieval finds the likely turns

    BM25 and pgvector search together, then a reranker orders the turns.

    • rank 1
      Let’s ship the beta on Friday. t1
    • rank 2
      Okay, make that Monday. t4
    • rank 3
      Then QA needs two days, minimum. t3
  5. 5

    Agents search and check

    Code picks the specialists the question needs.

    • memoryA decision at t1, a correction at t4.
    • consistencyAn update, not a contradiction.
    • assistanceDrafts two findings.
  6. 6

    The guard drops what it cannot quote

    Code checks every finding against the turns it cites.

    • kept
      Maya moved the beta to Monday.
      quotes found in t1, t4
    • dropped
      QA will finish by Thursday.
      no such quote in t3
  7. 7

    The card

    The old date is struck through, with who changed it and where.

    FridayMonday
    Who
    Maya
    Where
    t1, t4
    Read as
    An update, not a contradiction
