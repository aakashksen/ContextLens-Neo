Desktop app · Windows

A copilot in your tray

Keep any call open. Press Ctrl Shift Space and a card floats on top with what was said and who said it.

Download for WindowsWatch the demo

In a call

  1. 1

    Join any call

    Zoom, Meet, Teams, anything. It hears your mic and the call audio.

  2. 2

    Press the hotkey

    Ctrl Shift Space works over any app. It also speaks up on its own when something changes.

  3. 3

    Read, then close

    The card stays on top until you close it. Pin it if you want it to stay.

Nothing leaves your machine

Every part of the pipeline runs on this computer. No account, no cloud, no telemetry.

Speech
whisper.cpp in a local container.
Model
A 4B model on your GPU, through Ollama.
Memory
PostgreSQL on this computer.
Forget
One button deletes the conversation.
