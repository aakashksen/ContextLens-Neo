# E7 questions from AMI annotations

`datasets/ami-questions/dev.json` is generated by `python -m contextlens.data.ami_questions --raw <folder holding ami/>`
(rule and code in `src/contextlens/data/ami_questions.py`). It reads local files only, uses no speech output and no model,
and gives the same file every time (seed 13).

Rule:
1. Only meetings in the `dev` split of `datasets/splits/ami.json`. Train and test meetings are never read.
2. AMI's manual abstractive summary has `decisions`, `actions` and `problems` sentences. A section qualifies for a meeting
   when at least one of its sentences is linked, by the corpus's summlink file, to a dialogue act whose first word falls
   inside a clean-transcript turn of that speaker. Only meetings where all three sections qualify are candidates (24 of 43).
3. Candidates are sorted by id and 11 are drawn with `random.Random(13)`; each gives one question per section (33 questions).
4. The question is a fixed sentence per section and names nothing from the answer. The gold answer is the section's linked
   summary sentences. The gold evidence is the turns holding the linked dialogue acts' first words. `as_of_seq` is the last
   of them, so all evidence is at or before it.
5. `labels_reviewed` is false until a person has read a sample (H2): `datasets/ami-questions/review-sample.md`, 15 questions
   drawn with seed 13.

Limits: the gold answer is an annotator's summary, not an exact span; a section's evidence is every turn the annotator
linked, so `recall@k` here means "a linked turn was cited". Evidence is the dialogue act's first word, not all its words.

Attribution: AMI Meeting Corpus, manual annotations v1.6.2, CC BY 4.0, https://groups.inf.ed.ac.uk/ami/corpus/. The
questions and gold answers are derived from its abstractive summaries and summary links.
