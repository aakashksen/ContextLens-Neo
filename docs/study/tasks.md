# Study tasks: four staged conversations

All four conversations are invented for this study. The names are made up, and none of the text comes from the
project's scenarios or from any real conversation. Show a participant only the conversation and its question,
never the answer key (`answer-key.md`). Each task is scored out of 2 points.

Task ids: `recall_a`, `recall_b`, `clarify_a`, `clarify_b`. In each pair, one conversation is done with the assistant
and the other alone; `python -m contextlens.experiments.e9_study plan N` says which, and in what order.

How a conversation is shown: the facilitator plays it into the assistant as text turns, and a participant working
alone reads it on paper. The question is asked after the last turn.

---

## `recall_a`: the allotment committee
Question: "Why did the committee rule out the second compost site, and what did they choose instead?"

1. **Odile:** Next item is where the big compost bins go. Two options on the table.
2. **Bram:** The first is the corner by the water tank. The second is behind the tool shed.
3. **Odile:** The shed site has better shade, so that's my pick.
4. **Tessa:** The shed site is where the delivery van turns round. Last spring it flattened the fence there.
5. **Bram:** Right, and the tank corner is flat and dry. It also keeps the smell away from plots one to six.
6. **Odile:** Fine, then we rule out the shed. Tank corner it is.
7. **Tessa:** Who orders the bins?
8. **Bram:** I will. Three bins, the plastic kind.
9. **Odile:** Moving on to the open day.

## `recall_b`: the repair workshop
Question: "Why did the group decide against renting the unit on Quill Street, and what did they do instead?"

1. **Ines:** We need a place for the Saturday repair sessions. Quill Street has a unit.
2. **Calder:** It's twice what we pay now, and the lease runs a full year.
3. **Ines:** The church hall is cheaper, but it's only free on the first Saturday of the month.
4. **Pavel:** What if we ask the hall for the first and third Saturdays?
5. **Calder:** The lease is the problem for me. If sign-ups drop in winter, we can't get out of it.
6. **Ines:** Agreed. No to Quill Street.
7. **Pavel:** I'll ask the hall about two Saturdays a month and report back.
8. **Ines:** Good. Next, the tool donations.

## `clarify_a`: the harvest supper
