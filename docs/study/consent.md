# Consent form for the ContextLens Neo informal user test

**DRAFT for the person to review. This text has not been reviewed by a lawyer or by UCSB's IRB, and it is not
legally or IRB approved.** It is written for informal product testing. If the results will ever be published as
research, check with UCSB's IRB first and replace this form with what they require. Fill in the bracketed parts
before use.

## What this is
ContextLens Neo is a conversation assistant that answers questions about what was said earlier in a conversation.
We are trying it with a few people to see whether it helps. You will do four short tasks about invented
conversations, two with the assistant and two without. It takes about [30] minutes. The conversations are made
up. You will not be asked to share anything about yourself or your own conversations.

## What is recorded
- A participant code (for example P03), not your name.
- For each task: which task it was, whether you had the assistant, and how many points you got.

## What is not recorded
- Your name, contact details, age, or anything that identifies you.
- Audio, video, or photos. Nothing is recorded from your voice or your screen.
- Any health or personal information. We do not ask about diagnosis, health, or background. If you mention any of
  that during the session, it is not written down.
- Written comments about you. If you give feedback aloud, it is not tied to your code.

## Taking part is your choice
- You do not have to take part, and you can say no without giving a reason.
- You can stop at any point, skip any task, or ask to leave a task unfinished. Nothing bad follows.
- You can ask questions before, during, or after.

## How to withdraw
You will be given a slip with your participant code. Tell [the person's contact: ____] your code at any time
before [date: ____] and your results rows are deleted from the results file and from any copy of it. Because
the results hold only the code, we cannot find your rows without it; if you lose the slip, we cannot withdraw
them. If results were already combined into a summary, a summary written before your withdrawal is redone without
you; anything already shared outside this project cannot be recalled.
