# Answer key (facilitator only)

Two points per task. Give 1 point for each key point the participant states, in their own words. Do not score
style or speed. A point is not given for an answer that adds a wrong claim to the right one.

## `recall_a`
- The shed site was ruled out because the delivery van turns round there and flattened the fence last spring (turn 4).
- They chose the corner by the water tank (turns 5 and 6; it is flat and dry and keeps smell from plots one to six).

## `recall_b`
- Quill Street was rejected because of the year-long lease, which they could not leave if winter sign-ups dropped (turn 5);
  the higher rent (turn 2) is also acceptable as the reason.
- Instead Pavel will ask the church hall for two Saturdays a month (turns 4 and 7).

## `clarify_a`
- The headcount changed: forty (turn 3) became fifty-five (turns 8 and 9).
- No one said the number was changing; the bakery was to be told fifty-five, but the earlier plan was forty (turn 7).
  Give this point for stating both numbers, with the earlier one named.

## `clarify_b`
- The entry price changed: six pounds per person (turn 2) became eight pounds (turn 8).
- It was not announced as a change, and the poster would show the new price. Give this point for stating both
  prices, with the earlier one named.
